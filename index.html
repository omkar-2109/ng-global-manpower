Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our candidate coordination, employer allocations, and official embassy processing.

Does NG Global charge any upfront registration or agency fees?
No. NG Global charges zero upfront agency or registration fees. Once you submit your details, our career executive coordinates 1-on-1 with you on WhatsApp to review your trade background, understand your preferences, and identify suitable foreign employers tailored to your profile. Official visa application, consular biometrics, and medical tests are paid directly by you to authorized government embassies and medical clinics.
Why are some international jobs completely free with zero service charges?
A select few international positions have zero service charges because overseas principal companies fully sponsor the recruitment and hiring costs directly to us for those specific project quotas. In these specific cases, candidates do not pay any service charges.
How do official visa application and processing payments work?
Candidates pay all official visa application fees, GAMCA medical examinations, and consular biometric processing charges directly to the relevant foreign embassies, government portals, or authorized diagnostic clinics. You never pay government visa fees to personal accounts of unverified agents.
Why is the Gulf process so fast (1 to 15 days) compared to Western countries?
Gulf nations (UAE, Saudi, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman) use streamlined electronic e-Visa portals directly connected to employer corporate block visas. In contrast, European countries, the USA, and New Zealand require formal labor market certifications, consular interviews, and biometric physical passport submissions, which take approximately 90 days.
Are food, accommodation, and transport provided by the employer?
Yes! All contracts secured through NG Global include free bachelor accommodation, duty transportation, medical health insurance, and either free meals or a generous monthly food allowance on top of your base salary.
Can candidates with an ECR (Emigration Check Required) passport apply?
Absolutely. Both ECR and ECNR passport holders are eligible. Our documentation desk assists with complete e-Migrate clearance and embassy attestations so you travel 100% legally.
